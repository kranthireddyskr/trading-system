Saturday, May 23

Nashville Day — Music City

Full Day
~$125/person
5–10 AM😴 Rest / Hotel

Catch a few hours of sleep. Check-in, freshen up. Don't rush — Nashville mornings are for recovery.

Free
10:00 AM🥞 Brunch — Pancake Pantry or Biscuit Love

Nashville's two iconic brunch spots. Pancake Pantry (since 1961) for classic stacks; Biscuit Love for trendy Southern biscuits and bonuts. Expect a wait — worth every minute.

📍 Pancake Pantry: 1796 21st Ave S · Biscuit Love: 316 11th Ave S

~$20/person Open in Maps ↗
2:00 PM🎵 Country Music Hall of Fame

The ultimate country music experience. Book online in advance to skip lines. Plan 2 hours. Rotating exhibits include Elvis, Johnny Cash, and Taylor Swift archives.

📍 222 Fifth Ave S, Nashville, TN 37203

~$30/person Open in Maps ↗
4:30 PM🏛️ Parthenon — Centennial Park

Full-scale replica of the Athens Parthenon. Great outdoor walk before dinner. The interior houses a 42-foot Athena statue. Park entry is free.

📍 2500 West End Ave, Nashville, TN 37203

Free (park) · $6–10 inside Open in Maps ↗
6:00 PM🍗 Dinner — Hattie B's Hot Chicken (Midtown)

Nashville's legendary hot chicken. Order one heat level below what you think you can handle — "Damn Hot" is a very different experience than "Medium." The sides (pimento mac, black-eyed pea salad) are elite.

📍 112 19th Ave S, Nashville, TN 37203

~$20/person Open in Maps ↗
7:30 PM🎵 Honky Tonk Highway — Lower Broadway

Hit Tootsie's Orchid Lounge, Robert's Western World, Losers Bar, and Legends Corner. Free to enter everywhere — live music at every stop. This is Nashville at its finest.

📍 Lower Broadway, Nashville, TN 37201

~$40–50/person (drinks) Open in Maps ↗
Sunset🌅 Rooftop Bar

L.A. Jackson rooftop at Thompson Hotel or Assembly Food Hall for panoramic views of the Cumberland River at golden hour. Stunning backdrop for the group photo.

📍 L.A. Jackson: 401 11th Ave S, Nashville, TN

Included in Broadway budget

Sunday, May 24

Nashville → Memphis — Blues & BBQ

~210 mi drive
~$120/person
11:00 AM🚗 Depart Nashville → Memphis

Check out of hotel. I-40 West, easy 3-hour Sunday drive. Grab a quick coffee before heading out.

Nashville, TN → Memphis, TN via I-40 W (~210 mi)

Free
~2:00 PM🏨 Arrive Memphis — Hotel Check-In

Drop bags at hotel. Book near Downtown to walk to all evening spots.

Hotel: $40/person/night
2:30 PM✊ National Civil Rights Museum — Lorraine Motel

One of the most powerful museums in America, built at the site where Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was assassinated on April 4, 1968. Plan 2 full hours. Deeply moving — go in ready to feel it.

📍 450 Mulberry St, Memphis, TN 38103

~$18/person Open in Maps ↗
5:00 PM🎙️ Sun Studio Tour

Where Elvis, Johnny Cash, Jerry Lee Lewis, and B.B. King recorded. The actual microphone Elvis first sang into is still there. 50-minute guided tour — book in advance.

📍 706 Union Ave, Memphis, TN 38103

~$15/person Open in Maps ↗
7:00 PM🍖 Dinner — Central BBQ

Memphis-style dry-rub BBQ perfection. Get the ribs, pulled pork, and smoked wings. Multiple locations — the Downtown spot is a quick walk from Beale Street.

📍 147 E Butler Ave, Memphis, TN 38103 (Downtown)

~$25/person Open in Maps ↗
8:30 PM🎷 Beale Street — Live Blues

The home of the blues. Walk the pedestrian strip and hit B.B. King's Blues Club, Rum Boogie Café, and Blues City Café. Free street access; small covers inside venues.

📍 Beale St, Memphis, TN 38103

~$35/person (drinks + covers) Open in Maps ↗
